### About me

I'm a 16-year-old developer from Poland with a simple philosophy: **if you can't find what you need — build it yourself**.  

I turn ideas into real mobile apps, web platforms, and AI tools — mostly for fun, sometimes for actual users. Right now I'm focused on polishing **[oskarminor.com](https://oskarminor.com)** and shipping new mobile & AI projects.

**Fun fact:** I love breaking things just to understand how they work.

---

### What I'm building right now

- **Omni** — AI-powered personal assistant that finds lost files, installs apps, manages your calendar & email using voice (co-founder, launching early 2026)
- **czytaj dalej** — Social book-sharing platform connecting you with real recommendations from your neighbors (not algorithms)
- **I Am Dog** — Interactive mobile game/simulator [](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.ronimstudio.iamdog)
- **streść.to** — Mobile & web AI summarizer
- **Keepy** — Smart receipt & warranty manager
- **Motur** — Social platform for motorcycle enthusiasts
- **SafeLabs** — Social safety project (presented at public events)
- …and a few more secret projects in the drawer

All projects are live on **[my portfolio → oskarminor.com](https://oskarminor.com)**
